MIXFORGE

One room, up to 4 DJ seats. Deck 1 is the room's shared deck -- whoever's turn it is queues a YouTube URL, everyone in the room hears the same download play back together. Decks 2-4 are yours alone: load a demo loop, a local file, or practice and sample. Everything dj.html does -- full 4-deck mix, waveforms, 16-pad MIDI sampler -- works here too. Browsers need a click before they will play sound.

MIDI sampler

Pads answer MIDI notes 36–51 (C2–D#3, any channel) — or the keys 1234 QWER ASDF ZXCV. Sample off any deck, including deck 1 (the room's currently-playing track) or a local file loaded into any deck. Notes outside the pad bank play the chromatic pad (outlined) pitched in exact 12-TET from its root; the pitch wheel bends it ±2 semitones. CC on MIDI channels 1–4 drive decks 1–4: CC7 fader, CC10 pan, CC74 filter, CC1 pitch, CC2 trim, CC8 crossfader.
